# Spec 001: Three-storey apartment building

Synthetic test specification written for CodeCheck AI evaluation. Not a real project.

## Fire separations and smoke alarms

Suites are separated from each other and from the common corridor by fire separations with a fire-resistance rating of 30 min. Smoke alarms are installed in every dwelling unit. Each dwelling unit has one smoke alarm, mounted on the living room ceiling; there are no smoke alarms in the bedrooms.

## Sound control

Walls between dwelling units are built to an STC rating of 45.

## Stairs and handrails

The two common exit stairs are 1 100 mm wide. Risers on the common exit stairs are 190 mm high. Handrails on the common exit stairs are mounted 900 mm above the stair nosings.

## Balconies

Balconies on the second and third storeys, about 3 m and 6 m above finished ground level, have glass guards 900 mm high.

## Exits

The building is not sprinklered; each floor area has two exits, and the travel distance to the nearest exit is up to 38 m.

Ground-floor units are two-storey townhouse-style units with bedrooms on the upper level and their only egress door on the lower level, opening directly to the outside.

## Finishes

Kitchens have quartz countertops and stainless steel appliances. Exterior cladding is fibre cement panel in charcoal grey. Each unit has an in-suite laundry closet.
